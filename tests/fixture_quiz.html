IBPS Clerk Quant & Reasoning Practice Quiz

Directions (1-4): Solve the following questions.

1) What is 15% of 240?

A) 32

B) 36

C) 40

D) 45

Correct Answer: B) 36

Explanation: 15% of 240 = 240 × 0.15 = 36.

2. The simple interest on Rs.5000 at 8% per annum for 2 years is A) 750 B) 800 C) 850 D) 900

Answer: B

Solution: SI = P×R×T/100 = 5000×8×2/100 = 800.

Q3. In a code, if MONDAY is written as NPOEBZ, then SUNDAY is written as?

(A) TVOEBZ

(B) TVNEBZ

(C) TVOFBZ

(D) TTOEBZ

Ans: A

Explanation: Each letter shifts one place forward.

4) Find the missing number: 3, 7, 15, 31, ?

  • A) 62
  • B) 63
  • C) 64
  • D) 65

Correct option is (B).

Explanation: Rule x2+1: 31×2+1 = 63.
