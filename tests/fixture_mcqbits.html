TSPSC Group 2 Previous Year Questions – Indian Polity

1) Which Article of the Constitution deals with the Right to Education?

A) Article 19

B) Article 21A

C) Article 32

D) Article 44

View Answer

B) Article 21A

Explanation: Inserted by the 86th Amendment Act, 2002.
