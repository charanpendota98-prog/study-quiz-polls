The Postgraduate Institute of Medical Education & Research (PGIMER), Chandigarh has released an official notification inviting online applications for 243 posts of Nursing Officer.

  • Application mode: Online only, through www.pgimer.edu.in
  • Job location: PGIMER, Chandigarh

Notification PDF | Apply Online

PGIMER Nursing Officer Salary

Selected candidates will be placed at Pay Level-7 of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix, i.e., Rs. 44,900 – 142,400/-.

Age Limit

Age Limit: 18 to 35 years as on 03.10.2026

Application Fee: Rs. 1500 (UR/OBC), Rs. 800 (SC/ST)
