1. Which river is the lifeline of Telangana?

A) Ganga

B) Krishna

C) Yamuna

D) Godavari

Answer: D) Godavari

Explanation: The Godavari and Krishna are the two major rivers that flow through Telangana.

2. A and B are brothers. C and D are sisters. A is married to C. How is D related to B?

a) Sister-in-law

b) Daughter-in-law

c) Niece

d) Aunt

Answer: a) Sister-in-law

Explanation: A is married to C, who is the sister of D.
